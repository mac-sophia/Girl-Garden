# Girl-Garden
Welcome to Girl Garden! Select the fruit you would like to grow and begin by clicking the seed to grow and collect your fruits. 
Every 20 seeds clicked grows a fruit on the tree!

When your tree hits 5 fruits grown, it harvests into your basket.

Upgrades: Buy upgrades using fruits in your basket. Prices increase each time.

Rewards: Earn badges as you collect more fruits.

Happy Gardening!
